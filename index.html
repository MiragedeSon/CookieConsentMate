Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Overview

CookieConsentMate is a browser extension that automatically handles cookie consent banners according to the user's preferences. This policy explains what data the extension processes and your rights as a user.

Data collected

CookieConsentMate stores only the following data, exclusively in your browser's local storage:

  • Extension enabled/disabled state
  • Cookie consent preferences (analytics, marketing, personalization: on/off)
  • Behavior settings (auto-reject, hide banners, show badge: on/off)
  • Session counter of banners handled (reset on browser close)

All data is stored using the browser's built-in storage API. The extension author has zero access to your data at any time.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Browsing history or URLs of visited websites
  • IP address or location data
  • Personally identifiable information
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Crash or error reports

Third parties

CookieConsentMate makes no external network requests and has no third-party dependencies. Your data never leaves your browser.

Data retention

Data is stored until you uninstall the extension or click "Reset all" in the popup.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access: All data is visible directly in the extension popup
  • Rectify: Modify your preferences at any time in the popup
  • Erase: Click "Reset all" in the popup, or uninstall the extension

Since no data is transmitted to any server, there is no data to request from us beyond what is already visible in your browser.

Contact

For any privacy-related questions or general inquiries, contact us at cookieconsentmate@atomicmail.io

Changes to this policy

If this policy is updated, the new version will be published alongside the updated extension. Continued use of the extension after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
